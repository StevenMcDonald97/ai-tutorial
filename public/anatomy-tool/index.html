A SMALL STUDIO ROUTINE

From gesture to form.

  1. Choose a figure and pose. In Pose mode, click a joint marker to open its movement controls. Reset joint restores that joint; Undo pose reverses your last adjustment.
  2. Switch to Construction. Draw the rib cage, pelvis, and limb directions first.
  3. Select a region. Open an approved drawing sheet to compare its construction and developed forms.
  4. Return to the whole figure. Connect the part to neighboring forms.
  5. Change the light. Notice which planes face it before adding tone.

Bone and muscle views use adapted Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D meshes. Female anatomy is temporarily disabled while its remaining structures are reviewed. Neutral source anatomy is preserved; posed deformation is approximate. Construction remains a teaching mannequin. Joint contact and muscle contraction are not simulated. Use validated anatomical references alongside it.
