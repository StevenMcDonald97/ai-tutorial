Only skeletal, muscular, and selected joint models are included. No brain,
cranial nerve model, inner-ear model, kidney, or other separately credited
noncommercial organ asset from the broader source collection is included.

Adaptations: selected actual bone meshes without label/origin/insertion
highlights; selected muscles without fascia, bursa or tendon-sheath overlays;
selected cartilage and discs; transformed units and coordinates; welded
vertices; bounded mesh simplification; quantized normals; browser packing;
region assignments; approximate posing; optional female-proportion field.
Exact source revision, input hashes, and conversion details:
anatomy/PROVENANCE.json

The source is adult male reference anatomy. The female-proportion option is
NOT independently sourced or validated female anatomy. The neutral male
geometry retains the source's proportions, subject to the documented
conversion/simplification. Posing is an approximation, not a muscle or
joint-contact simulation. These models do not replace anatomical assessment.

REVISION 06 — FEMALE SOURCE CORRECTION
The male-proportion female adaptation described above is historical. It is no
longer rendered by the current anatomy viewer. Only the male Z-Anatomy atlas
is used for detailed muscle anatomy. Female-source skin and pelvis now come
from HuBMAP / Visible Human, CC BY 4.0; see torso/SOURCES.md for full credits.
The mapped male schemas are derivatives of the Z-Anatomy material under its
CC BY-SA terms. Female-pelvis schemas are HRA derivatives under CC BY 4.0.

REVISION 07 — FEMALE LOWER-LIMB EXTENSION
Female-source skeletal additions: HRA female v1.10, Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein / HuBMAP, CC BY 4.0. https://lod.humanatlas.io/ref-organ/united-female/v1.10
76 female lower-limb muscles: Thor E. Andreassen, Donald R. Hume, Landon D. Hamilton, Karen E. Walker, Sean E. Higinbotham and Kevin B. Shelburne (2023), Three Dimensional Lower Extremity Musculoskeletal Geometry, Scientific Data 10, 34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01905-2 ; https://digitalcommons.du.edu/visiblehuman/1/ ; CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Adapted through slorksmo/Human-Atlas, revision 5bb5713aab18d7fe9380c3339eb09f173491ea06: https://github.com/slorksmo/Human-Atlas . Its female-source muscles were fitted by anatomical group using rotation, uniform scale and translation, then simplified with quantized normals. Reported bone agreement is 17–36 mm. The assembly combines different female references; it is not a validated anatomical or biomechanical atlas.
Our changes: select 35 skeletal meshes and all 76 donor-muscle meshes, decode normals, translate into existing female pelvis coordinates and assign selectable regions. No borrowed male geometry is included. The original higher-resolution female pelvis and mapped landmarks are retained unchanged. Neutral pose only. See reference-bodies/female-provenance.json and FEMALE-SOURCE-ATTRIBUTION.md.
